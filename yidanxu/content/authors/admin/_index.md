## About Me

I am a 4th-year Ph.D. candidate in Statistics at the University of Michigan, where I work with Professor [Xuanlong Nguyen](https://dept.stat.lsa.umich.edu/~xuanlong/) and Professor [Yixin Wang](https://yixinwang.github.io). My research focuses on Causal Inference, Optimal Transport, and Generative Models. I hold an M.Sc. in Statistics from the University of Washington and a B.Sc. in Mathematics from Imperial College London. While at Imperial, I worked with Professor [Seth Flaxman](https://sethrf.com) on Gaussian Processes and Variational Autoencoders (VAEs).
